Our Story

Every love story is beautiful, but ours is our favourite.

Chapter I

A Chance Encounter

It was a quiet autumn afternoon in 2019 when Sarah walked into the small bookshop on Riad Street, searching for a novel she'd been chasing for months. She found it — and found Omar reaching for the exact same copy at the exact same moment. Their hands met over the spine of a story, and somehow, their own story began writing itself right there.

They talked for two hours. Then another two. Neither of them noticed the sun had set until the shopkeeper gently switched off the lights around them.

Chapter II

The Realization

Months passed, and with each one, a quiet certainty settled deeper into both their hearts. It wasn't one grand moment — it was thousands of small ones. A coffee left on the desk before the other woke. A laugh so familiar it felt ancient. A silence so comfortable it felt like home.

Omar once told his sister: "She makes the world feel like it's exactly the right size." Sarah told her mother: "He's the only place I've ever felt completely at ease."

Chapter III

The Question

On a warm July evening, under the stars above the hillside they'd walked on their third date, Omar stopped, turned to Sarah, and knelt on the very ground they'd sat on a hundred times before. He had no grand rehearsed speech — only the truest, simplest words he had ever spoken.

"Every version of my future starts with you in it. Will you be in all of them?"

She said yes before he had finished the sentence.
